Disaster Safety Guidelines

Official Sourced Protocols (NDMA / IMD)

Immediate Protocol for Assessed Zone:

🔴 Red Zone (High Risk) Protocol
  • Evacuation Readiness: If local authorities issue an evacuation advisory, move immediately along designated routes.
  • Vertical Shelter: Move to designated high ground or cyclone/flood shelters. Avoid basements and low-lying ground.
  • Utilities: Turn off main electrical switches and cooking gas cylinders before leaving.
  • Emergency Kit: Keep go-bag ready (drinking water, medicines, torch, dry food, power bank).
🟡 Yellow Zone (Moderate Risk) Protocol
  • Monitor Alerts: Keep tuned to official IMD/NDMA radio, TV, or weather channels.
  • Avoid Vulnerable Paths: Do not drive or walk through waterlogged underpasses or overflowing culverts.
  • Secure Property: Anchor outdoor loose items; check drainage openings around home.
🟢 Green Zone (Low Risk) Protocol
  • Maintain Preparedness: Check emergency supplies and first aid items.
  • Crucial: Low risk does not mean safe. Changing weather can rapidly shift hazard zones.

Flood & Heavy Precipitation Safety (IMD / NDMA)

  • Turn Around, Don't Drown: Just 15 cm (6 in) of moving water can knock an adult down, and 30 cm (1 ft) can float a vehicle.
  • Electrical Safety: Never touch downed power poles, electrical wires, or wet appliances.
  • Safe Drinking Water: Drink only boiled or chlorinated water. Flood waters carry dangerous contaminants.
  • Livestock & Pets: Untie animals so they have a chance to swim to safety if waters rise.

Earthquake Safety (NDMA Protocol)

  • DROP, COVER, AND HOLD ON: Drop onto hands and knees, cover head and neck under sturdy desk or table, and hold on until shaking stops.
  • Stay Indoors: Do not rush to exits. Stay away from glass windows, exterior walls, and heavy light fixtures.
  • Never Use Elevators: Power may fail; use stairwells once shaking ends.
  • If Outdoors: Move into clear open spaces away from buildings, overhead cables, and chimney stacks.

Cyclone & High Wind Safety (IMD Protocol)

  • Stay Inside: Remain indoors on the lowest floor in an interior room without exterior windows.
  • Beware of the Eye of the Storm: If the wind suddenly dies down, the cyclone eye may be passing. Extreme winds will resume shortly from the opposite direction.
  • Unplug Electronics: Disconnect non-essential electronics to protect against lightning surges.
Official Sourced References:

National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA), Government of India Guidelines (ndma.gov.in) and India Meteorological Department (IMD) Standard Operating Procedures for Disaster Management.
